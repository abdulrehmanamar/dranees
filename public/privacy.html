Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Commitment to patient confidentiality, medical record security, and clinical ethics in accordance with healthcare privacy standards.

Last Updated: October 2024

1. Information Collection

We collect personal and medical information strictly necessary to provide specialized cardiac surgical consultations and care. This includes:

  • Patient identification details (Name, Contact Information, Age, Emergency Contacts).
  • Comprehensive medical history, including prior surgeries, medications, and family cardiac history.
  • Diagnostic reports, imaging (ECGs, Echocardiograms, Angiograms), and laboratory results.
  • Communication records regarding consultations and treatment planning.

2. Data Usage & Processing

The information collected is strictly utilized for medical purposes to ensure the highest standard of care:

  • Formulating personalized surgical or conservative treatment plans.
  • Coordinating care with referring cardiologists or hospital surgical teams.
  • Administrative processing, including appointment scheduling and post-operative follow-up.
  • Anonymized data may be used for clinical audit and medical research, strictly adhering to medical ethics guidelines.

3. Patient Confidentiality & Security

We employ robust technical and organizational measures to safeguard your health information against unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure.

  • All digital records are encrypted and stored on secure servers compliant with medical data protection standards.
  • Access to patient files is restricted to authorized medical personnel on a strict 'need-to-know' basis.
  • Physical diagnostic files are securely archived in access-controlled environments.

4. Information Disclosure

We never sell, trade, or rent your personal health information. Disclosure is limited to:

  • Other healthcare professionals directly involved in your care, with your explicit or implied consent.
  • Legal authorities when mandated by statutory law or court order.
  • Authorized family members or designated representatives as specified in your consent forms.
