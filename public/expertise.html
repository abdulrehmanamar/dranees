Revascularization On-Pump & Off-Pump

Coronary Artery Bypass Grafting (CABG)

Surgical revascularization for patients with multi-vessel coronary artery disease or left main stem stenosis. We utilize the Left Internal Mammary Artery (LIMA) to bypass the left anterior descending (LAD) artery, yielding gold-standard 15-year graft patency, alongside radial artery or saphenous vein grafts for additional target vessels.

Operating Time 3 – 5 Hours
Hospital Stay 5 – 7 Days
Full Recovery 6 – 8 Weeks
Valvular Heart Disease Repair First Strategy

Aortic & Mitral Valve Surgery

Reconstruction or replacement of diseased aortic, mitral, and tricuspid valves. We emphasize mitral valve repair (annuloplasty rings, chordal reconstruction) to maintain ventricular geometry. For severe aortic stenosis, modern bioprosthetic tissue valves or long-lasting mechanical valves are precisely implanted.

Operating Time 3 – 4 Hours
Hospital Stay 5 – 7 Days
Full Recovery 6 – 8 Weeks
Aortic Pathology Root & Aneurysm Reconstruction

Aortic Aneurysm & Root Reconstruction

High-complexity surgical management of ascending aortic dilation, root aneurysms, and acute Type A dissections. Techniques include composite valve graft replacement (Bentall Procedure) and valve-sparing root reimplantation (David Procedure) with meticulous coronary reimplantation.

Operating Time 4 – 6 Hours
Hospital Stay 7 – 10 Days
Full Recovery 8 – 12 Weeks
Minimally Invasive Keyhole Access & Septal Closures

Minimally Invasive & Adult Congenital Repairs

Right mini-thoracotomy incisions sparing median sternotomy for candidate valve surgeries and Atrial Septal Defect (ASD) repairs. We also perform adult congenital reconstructions for Ventricular Septal Defects (VSD) and aortic coarctation with minimal surgical trauma and expedited functional rehabilitation.

Operating Time 2.5 – 4 Hours
Hospital Stay 3 – 5 Days
Full Recovery 3 – 4 Weeks
Complex Surgery Secondary Re-entry Protocols

Complex Cardiac Re-operations (Redo)

Specialized surgical interventions for patients requiring secondary or tertiary cardiac procedures. Managed with pre-operative high-resolution 3D CT mapping, oscillating saw re-sternotomy, peripheral cannulation safety protocols, and experienced management of severe mediastinal adhesions.

Operating Time 4 – 7 Hours
Hospital Stay 7 – 12 Days
Full Recovery 8 – 12 Weeks

The Surgical Care Pathway

Every patient journey follows an evidence-based clinical pathway designed to maximize safety, reduce anxiety, and optimize recovery.

01

Diagnostic Evaluation

Comprehensive review of angiograms, 2D/3D echocardiograms, cardiac CT, and EuroSCORE II surgical risk stratification.

02

Surgical Execution

Meticulous operative precision under continuous intra-operative transesophageal echocardiography and dedicated cardiac anesthesia.

03

ICU & Step-Down

Protocolized hemodynamic management, early extubation within 6 hours, and step-down transition by day 2.

Cardiovascular rehabilitation program, nutritional guidance, and milestone follow-ups at 2 weeks, 6 weeks, and 3 months.
